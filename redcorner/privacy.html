01Who is responsible

This policy covers the Android app Red Corner - Game Space ("the App"), published by Fahreza Raditya Busan, operating as FRB Project ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the data processing described here. Contact details are in section 14.

02Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location, and the App has no user accounts.
  • The App and the services it uses collect identifiers (your User ID, advertising ID, and app instance ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app activity, and diagnostics.
  • Some of this data is shared with advertising partners to show and measure ads. See Google Play Data safety categories.
  • Your app library, the launcher, and the App's personalization features work on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal data for money. Some laws treat sharing data with ad partners as a "sale" or "sharing"; you can opt out as described in section 7.

04Data that stays on your device

  • App library: the list of installed apps you add to the App is read from your device and stored only in the App's private storage.
  • Usage Access: used only to detect when an app in your library is opened, so the App can apply the personalization you chose. App usage history is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Content displayed over other apps: displayed and processed on the device. The App does not modify other apps or access their data.
  • Elevated access: system-level permissions you grant yourself, for example through wireless debugging. They are used only to run the device actions you start, and those actions are visible through a foreground service notification.
  • Settings, profiles, and your acceptance of the Terms: stored in the App's private storage and deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

06Why we process data

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide and verify the premium features you paid for.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the App stable and secure (crash reports), to understand usage in aggregate (analytics), and to deliver update notices.
  • Consent: for personalised advertising and the related use of your advertising ID where consent is legally required. You can withdraw consent at any time.

07Ads and consent

Users in the EEA, the UK, and other regions where it is required are shown a consent form before personalised ads are served. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path may differ by device). A premium subscription removes ads entirely.

08Retention

  • Premium access records are kept while the access is valid, and removed on request or when no longer needed.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept according to Firebase's retention settings, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Subscription records are kept by Google Play as required for billing and tax purposes.
  • Data stored only on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

09Your rights and deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

How to make a request: email us at the address in section 14 and include the User ID shown on the Profile page of the App. For data we control, such as premium access records linked to that ID, we will act on your request and aim to respond within 30 days.

Data held by third parties: data collected by advertising partners and Google Firebase is held under their own policies and is not linked to an account with us, so we cannot delete it on your behalf. You can limit it by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, declining personalised ads, or using the controls offered by those providers in section 5.

Because the App has no accounts, uninstalling it removes all data stored on your device. Requests about your User ID do not cancel an active Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play first.

10International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on safeguards offered by the providers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11Security

Data sent to our providers is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit the data we process to what the App needs.

12Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 is using the App, contact us with the User ID shown on the Profile page, and we will remove the premium access records linked to it. Data held by advertising partners and Google Firebase is handled as described in section 9.

13Changes

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. The latest version is always on this page with its effective date. Material changes will also be announced in the App.
